---
title: "Project 168: Launching the New Standard"
---

With the first bodybuilding show officially in the books, it's time to transition from reacting to the finish line to establishing a new direction. 

Enter **Project 168**.

## Mission Statement

> Build the strongest, most muscular version of myself at approximately 168 pounds. Prioritize quality muscle, strength, health, and consistency while maintaining a physique I'm proud of year-round.

The philosophy behind Project 168 is less about hitting a random scale weight and more about setting a standard: **Can I weigh 168 pounds and look like I lift every single day?** It’s a process-oriented challenge built for long-term growth. The contest gave me a finish line; Project 168 gives me a direction.

---

## Physique Goals
Instead of focusing purely on scale weight, progress will be judged on visual standard improvements:

* **Improve:**
  * ✅ Upper chest fullness
  * ✅ Wider back
  * ✅ Thicker upper back
  * ✅ More hamstring detail
  
* **Maintain:**
  * ✅ Round delts
  * ✅ Tight waist
  * ✅ Quad sweep

---

## Performance Goals
These are the milestones I'll be chasing over the next 6–12 months:

* **20 strict pull-ups**
* **135 lbs x 8 Weighted Dips**
* **405 lbs x 8 Hack Squat**
* **405 lbs x 8 Trap Bar Deadlift** 
* **+45 lbs x 8 Weighted Pull-ups** 
* **225 lbs x 8 Incline Smith Press**
* **800 lbs x 8 Leg Press**
* **150 lbs x 8 Overhead Press** 

---

## Lifestyle & Recovery Goals
Building a standard requires consistency in the daily habits that power growth:

* **Nutrition:** Hit macros at least 90% of the time. (One weekend doesn't define the year!)
* **Cardio:** 30 minutes, 4–5 days/week. Enough to stay cardiovascularly healthy without interfering with muscle growth.
* **Recovery:** 
  * Average 7.5+ hours of sleep per night
  * Walk daily
  * Manage and minimize lifestyle stress

---

## Timeline

* **August: Recovery & Habits**
  * Recover from prep fatigue.
  * Establish daily habits.
  * Dial in transition/maintenance macros.
* **September: Overload**
  * Focus on progressive overload.
  * Begin climbing the strength curve.
* **October: Exceed Prep**
  * Push lifts to exceed prep strength numbers.
  * Document visual progress with photos every two weeks.
* **Winter: Build Phase**
  * Enter a dedicated building phase.
  * Maintain weight around 168–172 lbs.
  * Zero tolerance for unnecessary fat gain.
* **Spring: Evaluation**
  * Evaluate progress.
  * Decide whether to plan another show prep or continue building.
